Thinking About Going Solar?

Choosing a solar system is an important decision. Explore these answers to better understand your options before starting your solar journey.

Ask Us a Question ↗
✦ Need help with your specific requirements? Our team can discuss the next steps with you.
01 What is a rooftop solar system?
A rooftop solar system uses solar panels installed on a suitable roof to generate electricity from sunlight. The electricity can be used by your home or business, depending on the system configuration.
02 Can solar panels reduce my electricity bill?
Yes, a properly sized solar system can reduce the amount of electricity you purchase from the grid. Actual savings depend on your consumption, solar generation, tariff, fixed charges and applicable metering rules. A zero bill is not guaranteed.
03 Is a government subsidy available for rooftop solar?
Eligible residential consumers may qualify for support under applicable government rooftop solar schemes. Eligibility, subsidy amounts, documentation and application requirements must be checked against current official guidelines. Commercial and industrial projects may have different eligibility rules.
04 What is an on-grid solar system?
An on-grid solar system is connected to the electricity grid. It can help offset grid electricity consumption. Net metering or other export arrangements depend on the applicable DISCOM rules and approvals. Standard on-grid inverters generally shut down during a grid outage.
05 Will my solar system work during a power cut?
A standard grid-connected system generally does not supply power during a grid outage for safety reasons. Backup power requires a suitable hybrid or backup configuration and compatible battery or equipment.
06 How do I choose the right solar system capacity?
System capacity depends on your electricity consumption, available rooftop area, shading, connection details, budget and applicable technical requirements. Your recent electricity bills are a useful starting point for planning.
07 How long does solar installation take?
The installation timeline varies with system capacity, site conditions, equipment availability and project scope. Documentation, utility approvals, inspection and commissioning can require additional time.
08 Do solar panels require regular maintenance?
Solar panels generally need periodic inspection and cleaning when dust or dirt affects performance. Inverter indicators, wiring, mounting structures and electrical protection should also be checked according to manufacturer guidance and appropriate safety procedures.
09 What warranty comes with a solar system?
Warranty coverage varies by component and manufacturer. Solar modules, inverters, mounting structures and workmanship may have different warranty terms. Always review the written warranty documents and exclusions in your quotation.
10 Can solar be installed for shops and industries?
Yes. Rooftop solar may be suitable for shops, offices, commercial buildings, warehouses and industrial facilities. Feasibility depends on electricity demand, roof suitability, connection capacity and applicable regulations.
