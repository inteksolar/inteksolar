COMMON QUESTIONS

Solar Energy FAQs

A few things to understand before moving ahead with rooftop solar.

Will solar make my electricity bill zero?

Not necessarily. Your final bill can still include fixed charges, minimum charges, grid imports and other applicable fees. The result depends on system generation, usage and your electricity tariff.

Does solar generate electricity on cloudy days?

Solar panels can generate electricity in diffuse sunlight, but output is generally lower than on sunny days. Generation also varies with shading, temperature and system conditions.

Will an on-grid solar system work during a power cut?

A standard grid-tied inverter usually shuts down during a grid outage for safety. Backup requires a suitable backup or hybrid configuration and compatible storage or equipment.

Is a government subsidy available for solar?

Eligible residential consumers may qualify for government rooftop solar support under applicable schemes. Eligibility, amounts and application procedures can change, so check the current official scheme and DISCOM requirements.

How do I know what system capacity I need?

Start with your recent electricity bills, daytime usage, available roof area and connection details. A site assessment can help establish an appropriate system size.
