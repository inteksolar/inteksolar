WHO WE ARE

Introducing Intek Solar Systems

Intek Solar Systems is a solar business brand of Intek Technologies, a GST-registered proprietorship. We focus on helping customers explore residential, commercial and industrial rooftop solar solutions.

Based in the Valsad–Vapi region of Gujarat, our approach is to understand each customer's energy needs, explain suitable system options and support informed project decisions.

  • ✓Solutions tailored to property and electricity requirements
  • ✓Guidance on solar equipment and system planning
  • ✓Support with understanding applicable solar processes
  • ✓Clear communication from enquiry to project planning
