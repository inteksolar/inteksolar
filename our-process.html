OUR WORKFLOW

A Clear Process from Enquiry to Handover

Each solar project is different. The steps below explain our typical project workflow; the exact sequence can vary depending on site conditions, approvals and project scope.

01
STEP ONE

Initial Enquiry & Discussion

We begin by understanding your property, electricity consumption, goals and approximate budget. This helps us identify which solar options may suit your needs.

  • Electricity bill discussion
  • Residential or commercial needs
  • Initial project requirements
02
STEP TWO

Site Assessment & Feasibility

Where required, the site is assessed for usable roof area, shading, structure, electrical connection and other factors that affect system design.

  • Roof suitability
  • Available installation area
  • Technical feasibility
03
STEP THREE

System Design & Component Selection

The proposed system is planned around estimated energy needs, available space, inverter and module compatibility, electrical protection and applicable requirements.

  • Capacity planning
  • Module and inverter options
  • Mounting and electrical components
04
STEP FOUR

Quotation & Project Confirmation

You receive a quotation outlining the proposed equipment, project scope, price and relevant terms. The project moves forward after you review and confirm the agreed details.

  • Quotation review
  • Scope and payment terms
  • Customer confirmation
05
STEP FIVE

Documentation & Applicable Approvals

The required documentation and utility application steps are identified according to the project type and current DISCOM rules. Subsidy eligibility is checked separately where applicable.

  • Required documents
  • Utility application process
  • Applicable subsidy guidance
06
STEP SIX

Installation & Electrical Work

Once the required arrangements are in place, the agreed installation work can proceed, including mounting, module installation, inverter setup, wiring and protection.

  • Mounting structure
  • Solar modules and inverter
  • Electrical safety components
07
STEP SEVEN

Inspection, Commissioning & Handover

The system proceeds through the applicable testing, inspection, utility approval and commissioning steps. The customer can then review the system operation, monitoring arrangements and product documentation.

  • Applicable testing and approvals
  • Monitoring guidance
  • Warranty documentation

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Before We Get Started

How do I start a solar project?

Contact us with your property type and recent electricity bill. We can discuss your requirements and identify the next steps for assessment and quotation.

Is a site visit always necessary?

A site assessment may be required to confirm roof suitability, shading, installation conditions and electrical requirements. The exact approach depends on the project.

How long does installation take?

The timeline varies with system size, site conditions, material availability, documentation and required utility approvals. A realistic schedule should be discussed after the project is assessed.

Can I receive a government solar subsidy?

Some eligible residential rooftop solar projects may qualify under applicable government schemes. Eligibility, documentation and approval conditions must be checked using the current official guidelines.
